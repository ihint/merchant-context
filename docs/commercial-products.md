# Commercial products

Merchant Context keeps cached merchant resolution free under reasonable rate limits.

Payment does not change search or comparison order.

Payment does not hide unknown or negative facts.

## Fresh inspection

`refresh_merchant` is the first paid product.

The current price is a $0.01 test.

The service keeps price terms, verified payment, settlement, inspection result, and cached record as separate evidence.

The service cannot inspect or settle without `approved: true` and a valid x402 payment.

## Higher-volume access

The product contract can set request volume, uptime, latency, support, and retention terms.

No public service plan or service-level commitment is sold in this release.

## Merchant monitoring

The planned product watches published fields and reports stale or changed evidence.

It does not rewrite merchant facts without a sourced correction.

No monitoring subscription is sold in this release.

## Correction history and referral analytics

The event model separates `merchant_claimed`, `fact_corrected`, referrals, action starts, and verified completion.

A click or client beacon cannot prove completion.

No analytics subscription is sold in this release.

## Sponsored refresh pools

A merchant may fund fresh evidence under a written agreement.

Funding cannot change rankings, hide facts, or make a completion claim.

No sponsored pool is active in this release.

## Verified-action fees

A fee may apply only under a merchant agreement and only after a valid merchant receipt or supported protocol response.

Merchant Context does not become Merchant of Record or proxy checkout.

No verified-action fee agreement is active in this release.
